The Neuro NEDs Charter for Neuroinclusive Performance

Moving from awareness to action.

A practical commitment to better work, stronger performance and fairer opportunity. Easy to join. Clear about what it means. Strong enough to matter.

The Commitment

Five commitments. One senior sponsor.
One practical action. One annual review.

The Charter gives organisations an accessible way to begin or strengthen their approach to neuroinclusion. Every organisation starts from a different place. Every signatory commits to turn good intent into visible, accountable action, applied proportionately to its size, context, workforce and responsibilities.

1
Listen and create psychological safety
We will listen to neurodivergent people, involve them in relevant decisions and work towards conditions where people can discuss barriers, request support and contribute without unnecessary fear of judgement or disadvantage.
2
Remove avoidable barriers
We will consider neuroinclusion across recruitment, communication, work design, working environments, performance, development and progression.
3
Make support easier to access
We will use inclusive practices wherever possible and provide appropriate individual support and reasonable adjustments without creating unnecessary barriers to requesting help.
4
Equip managers to act
We will help managers set clear expectations, communicate effectively, hold useful conversations, implement agreed support and seek advice when an issue falls outside their role.
5
Lead, measure and improve
We will assign senior responsibility, select practical priorities, review relevant evidence and report on progress over time.
The minimum effective standard — an active signatory has
Five accepted commitments
One named senior sponsor
One practical action
One annual review
How The Charter Works

Simple to join. Meaningful to keep.

Joining should be simple. Keeping the commitment meaningful requires one small activation step and one light annual review. The Charter does not require every request to be approved or every difficulty to be removed. It requires fair consideration, better-informed decisions and action on avoidable barriers.

01 — JOIN
Two-minute registration
Complete the two-minute registration form and accept the five commitments.
02 — ACTIVATE WITHIN 30 DAYS
Name your sponsor
Name a senior sponsor and day-to-day contact, choose one practical action and set a target date.
03 — ACT
Use the free toolkit
Use the free Neuro Inclusion Toolkit and relevant resources to make progress on the selected action.

04 — REVIEW ANNUALLY
Reflect and reset
Complete a short progress review, reflect on what changed and select the next priority.
Choose One Practical Action

Starting small is acceptable.

Every signatory chooses at least one achievable action for its first 12 months. The action must be relevant, owned and capable of being reviewed. Organisations may select another action that advances one or more Charter commitments — purchasing a Neuro NEDs service is not a condition of signing.

Employee voice
Create or improve an accessible way for neurodivergent employees to contribute to relevant decisions.
Working practices
Introduce clearer standards for meetings, written actions, priorities or communication.
Manager capability
Give managers practical guidance, tools or applied development.
Support and adjustments
Review how employees request, receive and revisit workplace support.
Recruitment and onboarding
Review one stage for avoidable barriers and improve access.
Work design and environment
Review flexibility, cognitive load, sensory conditions or opportunities for job crafting.
Performance and progression
Review how expectations, feedback, development or promotion decisions are communicated and assessed.
Data and accountability
Establish a proportionate baseline, measure or reporting process.
Structured insight
Complete the Corporate NIIT or another suitable evidence-based organisational review.
Status That Means Something

Simple statuses. Not awards.

The Charter uses simple participation statuses. They are not awards or maturity ratings.

Registered
The organisation has joined and accepted the five commitments.
Next: complete activation within 30 days
Active signatory
A sponsor and contact are named and the first action has been selected.
May use the approved Charter Signatory mark
Progress reviewed
The annual review has been completed and the next priority selected.
Charter status continues for the next period

What signing means

  • A public commitment to the five Charter principles.
  • Named accountability through a senior sponsor and day-to-day contact.
  • At least one practical action pursued during each 12-month period.
  • A short annual review and a renewed priority.

What signing does not mean

  • That the organisation has been audited, certified or accredited by Neuro NEDs.
  • That every employee has the same experience or the organisation is fully neuroinclusive.
  • That signing provides legal assurance or proves compliance with every duty.
  • That every request must be approved or every difficulty can be removed.

Credibility, Governance And Review

Our commitments as Charter owner

The Charter is an applied Neuro NEDs framework informed by evidence, lived experience and organisational practice. It is designed to promote credible progress without creating an unnecessary reporting burden.

  • Neuro NEDs owns and maintains the Charter, signatory rules, marks and associated methodology.
  • The Charter and toolkit will be reviewed periodically as evidence, language, law and organisational practice develop.
  • Neuro NEDs may suspend or remove signatory status where an organisation materially misrepresents its status, does not activate or review its commitment, or seriously undermines the Charter's integrity.
  • Aggregated or anonymised Charter information will only be used with appropriate privacy information and safeguards.
The Charter and its resources provide general organisational guidance. They do not replace legal, clinical, occupational-health or other appropriately qualified professional advice.
